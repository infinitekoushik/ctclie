The choices we make, and you can change

Your payslip depends on how your employer splits your CTC, so some numbers are assumptions. We start from these:

  • Basic pay is 50% of CTC. You can pick 40% or 60%.
  • Your PF and your employer's PF are each capped at Rs 1,800 a month. Paying PF on more than the ceiling is voluntary, so some employers do and some do not.
  • Gratuity is 4.81% of basic and sits inside your CTC. It is not paid monthly.
  • Professional tax is Rs 2,400 a year.
  • When you enter rent, we assume your HRA is half of your basic pay.
  • A meal card is paid as vouchers, so it is tax-free up to the limit but it is not cash in your bank account.

Why calculators give different answers

In November 2026 we looked at four public calculators for a 50 LPA package. They gave between Rs 2,74,633 and Rs 2,91,048 a month. The gap, about Rs 16,000, comes from choices like the ones above: how much is basic, whether PF is capped, which state, which regime. None of that is a secret. We just show our choices so you can compare like with like.

What we leave out

India: LTA, health insurance under 80D, employer NPS, state-by-state professional tax, and any reimbursements. US: city taxes and health premiums. Canada: RRSP, employer benefits and Quebec, which has its own system. California and New York are estimates. UK, Australia and Singapore follow the 2026 rules but skip pension top-ups and similar extras.

Other countries: we checked the US, UK, Australia and Singapore in October 2026 against the IRS, Social Security Administration, California FTB, New York DTF, HMRC, gov.scot, ATO, IRAS and CPF Board. We are adding a link for each rule, as we did for India.
