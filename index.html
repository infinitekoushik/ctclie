How we calculate

What is in each result, and what is not

The numbers are estimates built from each country's published 2026 tax rules. Here is what we count and what we leave out.

CountryWhat we includeWhat we leave out
IndiaIncome tax (new or old regime, surcharge and cess), your PF and professional tax. CTC is first reduced by employer PF and a gratuity provision.HRA, LTA, home loan interest and other deductions. Professional tax is set at Rs 2,400 a year.
United StatesFederal income tax (2026 brackets and standard deduction), Social Security, Medicare, and state income tax for California, New York and Illinois. California SDI.401(k) and HSA contributions, health premiums, city taxes such as NYC, other state payroll programs and itemised deductions.
United KingdomIncome tax for England, Wales and Northern Ireland or for Scotland (2026/27 bands), the personal allowance taper and employee National Insurance.Pension contributions, student loans, tax codes and benefits in kind.
AustraliaResident income tax (2026-27 rates), the low income tax offset and the 2% Medicare levy.Employer super, HELP debt, the Medicare levy surcharge and other offsets.
SingaporeResident income tax, earned income relief and, for citizens and PRs, CPF contributions with CPF relief.Other reliefs and rebates, and the lower CPF rates for new PRs.
