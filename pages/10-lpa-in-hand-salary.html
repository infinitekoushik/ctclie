How to keep more of your 10 LPA

Pick the right regime
Compare regimes — the difference is often Rs.5,000–15,000/year at this level. Takes 10 seconds in the calculator above — often worth thousands per year.

NPS: Rs.50,000 extra deduction
Section 80CCD(1B) gives Rs.50,000 off taxable income beyond your 80C limit. At 10 LPA that saves Rs.2,500/year minimum in old regime.

Negotiate your structure
Higher Special Allowance, meal vouchers (Rs.2,200/month tax-free), LTA — all increase take-home without changing your CTC number. Ask HR.
