The honest pros and cons

🇮🇳 Staying in India
Family proximity — No 12-hour flights, aging parents nearby, roots intact
House help + lifestyle — Cook, cleaner, driver for what costs 10-20x in the US
Lower housing stress — Rs.50-80L buys a real flat. No $800K starter home
No visa anxiety — No H-1B lottery, no 10-year green card wait
Growing market — India's GDP trajectory creates real wealth-building opportunities
Tax efficiency — New regime zero tax up to Rs.12.75L. No state income tax in 9 states
🇺🇸 Moving to the USA
Absolute salary ceiling — FAANG salaries of $300K-$800K simply don't exist in India yet
Career credibility — US experience opens global doors that Indian experience sometimes doesn't
401(k) + RSUs — Equity compensation and retirement matching are standard. India is catching up slowly
Savings rate — Despite higher costs, absolute monthly savings in USD at senior levels is hard to match in India
Work culture — Better work-life balance, anti-discrimination protections and workplace rights in general
Dollar savings — Sending $1,000 home to India creates Rs.95,500. Powerful for family real estate investments
