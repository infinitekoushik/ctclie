How we worked this out

  • Basic is 50% of CTC. PF is 12% of basic, capped at Rs 1,800 a month.
  • Gratuity is about 4.81% of basic. Professional tax is Rs 2,400 a year.
  • New regime: Rs 75,000 standard deduction and the 87A rebate. Surcharge and 4% cess are included.
  • Old regime: Rs 50,000 standard deduction and Rs 1.5 lakh in 80C. HRA, LTA and home loan interest are not included unless you add rent in the main calculator.

At 30 LPA every extra rupee is taxed at 31.2%

On the new regime your taxable income at 30 LPA is about ₹28,31,285, well inside the 30% slab, which starts at Rs 24 lakh. You pay about ₹4,46,561 in income tax a year including cess, which is 14.9% of your CTC on average. But each extra rupee of taxable pay costs about 31.2% once cess is added, which is why a raise or bonus feels smaller than it looks.

Here is what that does to money you add. Going from 25 to 30 LPA adds Rs 5 lakh of CTC, but only about ₹3,38,679 a year reaches you, which is 68% of the raise. A Rs 3 lakh bonus leaves about ₹2,06,400, or 69% of it.

The Rs 50 lakh surcharge line is still far away. Your taxable income would need to rise by about ₹21,68,715 before surcharge starts, so at 30 LPA it matters only with a very large bonus or stock vest. The old regime does not help either: you would need about ₹6,47,600 of deductions beyond Rs 1.5 lakh in 80C for it to win.

Ways to keep more of it

🏦
Ask about employer NPS
Every Rs 50,000 of employer NPS contribution lowers your tax by about ₹15,600 a year at this slab.
🍴
Meal card, if you eat out anyway
Up to Rs 8,800 a month in vouchers saves about ₹32,947 a year in tax. Your cash falls by the card amount.
📈
Negotiate in after-tax terms
To take home Rs 1 lakh more a year you need about ₹1,48,929 more CTC at this level.
⚖️
Skip the old regime
You would need about ₹6,47,600 of deductions beyond Rs 1.5 lakh in 80C and the standard deduction for the old regime to come out ahead.
