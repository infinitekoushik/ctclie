About CTCLie

Your CTC is a lie.
We do the math.

CTCLie is a free, no-signup salary truth engine built for tech workers across the US, India, UK, Australia, and Singapore, so you never get surprised by your actual take-home again.

Why we built this

Salary negotiations are broken. A US company quotes you $180,000 total compensation and it sounds great until you realise $60,000 is RSUs vesting over 4 years, $20,000 is a signing bonus that claws back if you leave and after federal + state tax you're netting $78,000 a year in cash.

The same story plays out in India, where a 30 LPA offer turns into 18 LPA in-hand after PF, professional tax and the new regime's surprising bite at higher slabs. And in the UK, where the National Insurance cliff at £50,270 catches even experienced earners off guard.

CTCLie exists to answer one question honestly: what does this offer actually pay you, every month, in your bank account?

A note on accuracy

CTCLie uses the best publicly available tax data we can source. For India, we use FY 2026-27 slabs under the new regime. For the US, we apply federal brackets plus a state tax estimate. For UK, Australia and Singapore we follow the current tax year rules at time of writing.

That said we are not a licensed tax advisor. For decisions involving large sums, offer negotiations or cross-border moves, please verify figures with a qualified accountant or tax professional in your jurisdiction. Use CTCLie to understand the ballpark before you walk into that negotiation room.
